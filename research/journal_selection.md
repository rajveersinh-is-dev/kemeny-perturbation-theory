# Oxford University Press (OUP) Journal Selection and Policy Verification

This document provides a thorough, evidence-based evaluation of Oxford University Press (OUP) journals for publishing this research, verifying official author instructions, fee structures, and self-archiving policies.

---

## 1. Candidate OUP Journals Evaluated

### Journal 1 (Primary Recommendation): *Information and Inference: A Journal of the IMA*
- **Publisher:** Oxford University Press on behalf of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications (IMA).
- **Official URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/imaiai`
- **Author Guidelines & Policies URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/imaiai/pages/General_Instructions`
- **Publication Charges URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/imaiai/pages/open-access`
- **Scope & Readership:** High-level mathematical articles on information theory, data science, machine learning theory, network analysis, statistics, and optimization.
- **Article Format & Length:** Encourages manuscripts under 25 pages for expedited review; LaTeX submission with `.tex` and `.bib` source files preferred.
- **Publishing Model:** **Hybrid Journal**. Authors have the choice between standard subscription publishing and open access.
- **Mandatory Author Publication Fee:** **NONE ($0.00).**
  - *Policy Verification:* Standard publication under the subscription license carries **zero page charges, zero submission fees, and zero mandatory article processing charges (APCs)**.
  - *Official Wording Paraphrase:* Authors who publish via the standard license do not pay an Article Processing Charge. Only authors who choose optional immediate open access (CC BY license) pay an APC.
  - *Date Checked:* October 17, 2026.
- **Preprint and Public Repository Policy:**
  - *Author's Original Version (AOV / Preprint):* OUP explicitly permits authors to deposit the un-refereed author version (preprint) on personal websites, institutional repositories, subject repositories (such as arXiv), and public platforms like **GitHub at any time with no embargo**.
  - *Accepted Manuscript (AAM):* After peer review and acceptance, the accepted version may be archived subject to the journal's standard embargo.
- **Why this is the primary fit:** The rigorous mathematical theorems, spectral graph theory, and algorithmic optimization are a perfect scope match for the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications data science readership.

### Journal 2 (Secondary Candidate): *The Computer Journal*
- **Publisher:** Oxford University Press on behalf of BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT.
- **Official URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/comjnl`
- **Author Guidelines URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/comjnl/pages/General_Instructions`
- **Scope & Readership:** Theoretical computer science, algorithms, discrete mathematics, network systems, and information technology. Published across four sections (Section A: Computer Science Theory, Methods and Tools; Section B: Computer and Communications Networks and Systems; Section C: Computational Intelligence and Machine Learning; Section D: Security and Privacy).
- **Article Format:** LaTeX submissions via standard templates.
- **Publishing Model:** **Hybrid Journal**.
- **Mandatory Author Publication Fee:** **NONE ($0.00).**
  - *Policy Verification:* Standard subscription route has **zero mandatory author publication fees or page charges**. Authors have the option to pay an APC if they elect open access.
  - *Date Checked:* October 17, 2026.
- **Preprint / Public Repository Policy:** Fully compatible with OUP's Green Open Access policy permitting preprints on GitHub and arXiv.
- **Why this is a strong alternative:** Section A (Computer Science Theory, Methods and Tools) regularly publishes algorithmic breakthroughs in graph theory and network optimization.

---

### Journal 3 (Tertiary Candidate): *IMA Journal of Applied Mathematics*
- **Publisher:** Oxford University Press on behalf of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications.
- **Official URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/imamat`
- **Author Guidelines URL:** `https://academic.oup.com/imamat/pages/General_Instructions`
- **Scope & Readership:** Applied mathematics, differential equations, dynamical systems, linear algebra, and mathematical modeling of physical and complex systems.
- **Publishing Model:** **Hybrid Journal**.
- **Mandatory Author Publication Fee:** **NONE ($0.00).**
  - *Policy Verification:* Standard subscription publication route is free of author fees. Optional APC applies only for Gold Open Access.
  - *Date Checked:* October 17, 2026.
- **Preprint / Public Repository Policy:** Identical OUP Green Open Access policy permitting preprint deposition at any time without embargo.

## 3. Dissemination and Repository Staging Strategy

In accordance with Oxford University Press's official self-archiving policy:
1. The **Author's Original Version (AOV)**, including complete LaTeX sources, preprint PDF, and the full research software implementation, is fully authorized to be published openly in a public GitHub repository prior to formal submission.
2. Upon submission, the manuscript does not claim prior publication or formal endorsement by the journal.
3. No proprietary publisher formatting, copyrighted journal logos, or confidential peer reviewer correspondence will be uploaded.
4. All code, datasets, tests, figures, and research notes are distributed under permissive open licenses (MIT License for code; CC-BY 4.0 for manuscript text and documentation).
